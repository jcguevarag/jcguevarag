## 🧬 Profile & Vision

I am a **Senior AI Researcher** bridging the gap between complex physical systems and reliable, trustworthy artificial intelligence. I specialize in taking complex research from rapid Proofs of Concept (PoC) to scalable, regulatory-compliant industrial solutions.

Currently, I lead the technical foundation of an internal **AI Testing Lab** initiative at **DNV**, focusing on:

* **Agentic Systems**: Architecting multi-agent systems using ReAct-style reasoning and action loops (LangChain, Google ADK).
* **Enterprise AI & RAG**: Designing robust Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines via Azure AI Search and Vertex AI.
* **AI Assurance & Safety**: Developing comprehensive evaluation frameworks (e.g., DeepEval), execution pipelines, and AI-aware risk methodologies (CyberNEMO).
* **Regulatory Alignment**: Ensuring AI systems meet the stringent standards of the **EU AI Act**.
* **Cyber-Physical Security**: Secure co-simulation, adversarial testing, and malicious FMU development for digital twins.
